Confirmation Policy
Booking Receipt: We'll send a booking receipt/confirmation upon receiving your booking request.
Payment Confirmation:Any booking is confirmed by depositing an advance payment @ 30% amount of the Total Cost of the Package, after that company will process the booking.
Balance Payment has to be made in full advance before 10 days of the tour date.
Payments can be remitted through NEFT/ RTGS/ UPI/ Net Banking any of the following modes and is subject to realization.
Once we receive the payment you will receive an acknowledgment from us showing receipt and confirmation of payment.
Cancellation Policy
In the event of cancellation of tour/travel services due to any avoidable/unavoidable reason/s, we must be notified of the same in writing (email or letter).
Cancellation charges will be effective from the date of cancellation.
More than 45 days before Arrival 10% of the Total package cost.
More than 30-45 days before Arrival 25% of Total package cost.
10-30 days before Arrival 40% of Total package cost.
